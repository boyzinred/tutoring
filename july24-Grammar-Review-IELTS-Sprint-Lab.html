Previous Grammar Points

Review three earlier points: possessive nouns (student's, students', children's), whose vs. who's, and possessive adjectives/pronouns (my/mine, their/theirs, its/it's).
New grammar

Reflexive Pronouns

Reflexive pronouns end in -self or -selves and refer back to the subject: I taught myself; They introduced themselves. Use by + reflexive pronoun to mean “alone”: She traveled by herself. Common expressions include enjoy yourself, help yourself, believe in yourself, and take care of yourself.
New grammar

Another vs. The Other

Another means one more or one additional item from a larger group: I need another notebook. The other means the remaining one of two: I have two notebooks. One is blue; the other is red.
New grammar

Other, Others, The Other, and The Others

Use other + plural noun (other students) and others without a noun. Use the other + singular noun for the remaining one of two, and the others for all remaining members of a specific group.
