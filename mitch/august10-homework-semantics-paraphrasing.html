Before you start

Read for meaning, not matching words

Two sentences can use very different words and still mean the same thing. Two sentences can also use many of the same words but mean different things. Your job is to follow the idea.

1 · Make a tiny movie

Read the sentence and picture what is happening. Who is doing something? What happens? Why or when does it happen?

If two sentences create the same little movie in your mind, they probably have the same meaning.

2 · Watch the logic words

Small words can flip the meaning. Pay attention to words like not, never, only, before, after, because, but, more, less, and must.

Same nouns + different logic = different meaning.

3 · Cover the original words

After you understand a sentence, pretend you cannot see it. Ask yourself, “How would I tell this idea to a friend?”

Good paraphrasing changes the words but keeps the facts.

4 · Change one chunk at a time

You do not need to rewrite a whole sentence at once. Replace one phrase first, then check whether the sentence still means the same thing.

Meaning must stay. Words can change.
