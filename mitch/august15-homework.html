Reading Assignment 2

Review seven important reading skills with a short example. Then read a Grade 3 story and complete a timed comprehension assignment.

Grade 3 Skill review 10 questions 20 minutes
Before the assignment

What will be tested?

Read the mini example first. Each card shows what one reading skill means and how to use it.

Mini Example: The Wet Puppy

On Monday, Leo found a small puppy shivering under the school steps. He wrapped it in his jacket and called the office. Soon, the puppy's owner arrived.

Skill 1

The 5 W's

Find the important facts: who, what, when, where, and why.

Example: Who found the puppy? Leo. Where was it? Under the school steps.
Skill 2

Main ideas, details, and summaries

The main idea tells what the whole passage is mostly about. Details support it. A summary gives only the most important events.

Main idea: Leo helps a lost puppy. Detail: He wraps it in his jacket.
Skill 3

Sequencing

Put events in the order they happened. Look for words such as first, next, then, and finally.

Order: First Leo finds the puppy. Next he calls the office. Last, the owner arrives.
Skill 4

Elements of a story

Look for the characters, setting, problem, important events, and solution.

Problem: The puppy is lost and cold. Solution: Leo gets help from the office.
Skill 5

Making inferences

Use story clues and what you already know to figure out something the author does not say directly.

Inference: Leo is caring because he keeps the puppy warm and asks an adult for help.
Skill 6

Context clues

Use nearby words and events to figure out the meaning of an unfamiliar word.

Example: The puppy is “shivering,” and Leo wraps it in a jacket. Shivering probably means shaking from the cold.
Skill 7

Figurative language

Authors sometimes compare things to create a strong picture. A comparison using like or as is called a simile.

Example: “The rain tapped like tiny fingers” means the drops made a light tapping sound.
During the assignment, go back to the passage and look for evidence. Do not choose an answer only because it repeats a word from the question.
