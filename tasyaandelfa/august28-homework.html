Five traps to avoid

Do not saySay instead
thirty thousandsthirty thousand — hundred, thousand, million stay singular after a number
three thirty o’clockthree thirty — o’clock is only for an exact hour
She has ten years.She is ten years old — English uses be for age
three point fourteenthree point one four — digits after the point are said one by one
the two paragraphthe second paragraph — use an ordinal for position

Words that mean “not exact”

Keep these words in front of the number when the amount is only an estimate: about, around, roughly, approximately, nearly, almost, more than, over, as much as, several, dozens of, thousands of.

more than 30,000 eggs → “more than thirty thousand eggs” · about 100 toads → “about one hundred toads”
